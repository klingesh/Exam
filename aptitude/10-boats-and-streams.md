# 🔟 BOATS AND STREAMS

⏱️ **Study time: 30 minutes** | 🎯 **Typical questions: 1–2**

> 🟢 **This is the easiest topic in the whole syllabus.** There are literally **two formulas** and they're both common sense. Do not skip it — it's guaranteed marks for 30 minutes of work.

---

## 📖 PART 1 — THE ENTIRE CONCEPT IN ONE PICTURE

```
DOWNSTREAM  (with the current) →  the river HELPS you  →  you go FASTER  →  ADD
UPSTREAM    (against the current) → the river FIGHTS you → you go SLOWER → SUBTRACT
```

Let **b** = speed of the boat in still water, **s** = speed of the stream.

```
Downstream speed  =  b + s        (D)
Upstream speed    =  b – s        (U)
```

### And the two reverse formulas (memorize — these are what get asked)

```
                D + U                              D – U
Boat speed =  ─────────           Stream speed = ─────────
                  2                                  2
```

> 🧠 **Why these work:** D and U are just b+s and b–s. Add them → the s cancels, leaving 2b. Subtract them → the b cancels, leaving 2s. That's it. You could re-derive them in the exam in 10 seconds.

### 📖 The vocabulary
| The question says | It means |
|---|---|
| "in still water" / "in calm water" | **b** (the boat alone) |
| "speed of the current / stream / river" | **s** |
| "downstream" / "with the stream" / "along the current" | **b + s** |
| "upstream" / "against the stream" | **b – s** |

## 📖 PART 2 — WORKED EXAMPLES, STEP BY STEP

### 🔍 Example 1 — Forward direction
*A boat's speed in still water is 10 km/h and the stream flows at 2 km/h. Find its downstream and upstream speeds.*
```
Step 1: Downstream = b + s = 10 + 2 = 12 km/h
Step 2: Upstream   = b – s = 10 – 2 =  8 km/h
✅ Downstream 12 km/h, Upstream 8 km/h
```

### 🔍 Example 2 — Reverse direction ⭐
*A boat goes downstream at 16 km/h and upstream at 10 km/h. Find the speed of the boat in still water and the speed of the stream.*
```
Step 1: Boat speed = (D + U)/2 = (16 + 10)/2 = 26/2 = 13 km/h
Step 2: Stream speed = (D – U)/2 = (16 – 10)/2 = 6/2 = 3 km/h
✅ Boat = 13 km/h, Stream = 3 km/h

VERIFY: 13 + 3 = 16 ✔   13 – 3 = 10 ✔
```

### 🔍 Example 3 — From distances and times ⭐⭐ (most common form)
*A boat covers 30 km downstream in 3 hours and 24 km upstream in 4 hours. Find the speed of the boat and of the stream.*

```
Step 1: The question gives distances and times, not speeds.
        FIRST convert them into speeds.

        Downstream speed = 30 / 3 = 10 km/h
        Upstream speed   = 24 / 4 =  6 km/h

Step 2: Now apply the two formulas.
        Boat   = (10 + 6)/2 = 16/2 = 8 km/h
        Stream = (10 – 6)/2 =  4/2 = 2 km/h

✅ Boat = 8 km/h, Stream = 2 km/h

VERIFY: Downstream 8+2 = 10 → 30 km takes 3 hrs ✔
        Upstream   8–2 =  6 → 24 km takes 4 hrs ✔
```
> 🚨 **The #1 mistake:** plugging the *distances* (30 and 24) straight into the formula. **Always convert distance and time into a SPEED first.** Write "D = ?, U = ?" on your sheet before doing anything else.

### 🔍 Example 4 — Round trip time
*A boat's speed in still water is 8 km/h and the stream is 2 km/h. How long will it take to go 30 km downstream and come back?*
```
Step 1: Downstream speed = 8 + 2 = 10 km/h → time = 30/10 = 3 hours
Step 2: Upstream speed   = 8 – 2 =  6 km/h → time = 30/6  = 5 hours
Step 3: Total = 3 + 5 = 8 hours
✅ 8 hours

Note: the return leg takes much longer. The round trip is NEVER 2 × (30/8).
```

### 🔍 Example 5 — The ratio pattern ⭐
*A boat takes twice as long to go upstream as it does to go downstream (same distance). Find the ratio of the boat's speed to the stream's speed.*

```
Step 1: Same distance → time and speed are INVERSE.
        Upstream takes 2× the time  →  upstream speed is HALF the downstream speed.
        So D = 2U.

Step 2: Substitute the definitions.
        b + s = 2(b – s)

Step 3: Expand and solve.
        b + s = 2b – 2s
        s + 2s = 2b – b
        3s = b

Step 4: So b : s = 3 : 1

✅ Boat : Stream = 3 : 1
```
**General result worth remembering:**
```
If upstream time = k × downstream time, then    b : s  =  (k + 1) : (k – 1)
```
| Upstream takes | b : s |
|---|---|
| 2× as long | 3 : 1 |
| 3× as long | 4 : 2 = 2 : 1 |
| 4× as long | 5 : 3 |

### 🔍 Example 6 — The full round-trip puzzle ⭐⭐
*A man rows to a place 48 km away and returns, taking 14 hours in total. He finds that he can row 4 km downstream in the same time as 3 km upstream. Find the speed of the stream.*

```
Step 1: Decode the second sentence. "4 km down in the same TIME as 3 km up."
        Same time → distance is proportional to speed.
        So  D : U = 4 : 3

Step 2: Let D = 4k and U = 3k.

Step 3: Use the 14-hour total for the round trip.
        Time down + Time up = 14
        48/(4k) + 48/(3k) = 14

Step 4: Simplify each term.
        48/4k = 12/k
        48/3k = 16/k

        12/k + 16/k = 14
        28/k = 14

Step 5: k = 28/14 = 2

Step 6: So D = 4(2) = 8 km/h, U = 3(2) = 6 km/h

Step 7: Stream = (D – U)/2 = (8 – 6)/2 = 1 km/h
        (And the boat = (8 + 6)/2 = 7 km/h)

✅ Stream speed = 1 km/h

VERIFY: Down 48/8 = 6 hrs, Up 48/6 = 8 hrs, total 14 hrs ✔
```

## 📖 PART 3 — TWO EXTRA FORMULAS (nice to have)

### ① Average speed of a full round trip
```
                       (b + s)(b – s)          b² – s²
Average speed  =  ─────────────────────  =  ──────────
                            b                     b
```
*Boat 10, stream 2 → average = (100 – 4)/10 = 9.6 km/h. Not 10 — the current costs you time overall, even though it helps one way.*

### ② Boat speed from a round trip taking t hours over distance d each way
```
      d       d
     ───  +  ───  =  t
     b+s     b–s
```
Just set it up and solve — that's what Example 6 did.

## ⚡ SHORTCUTS & SPEED TRICKS

1. **Write D and U at the top of your rough sheet, every single time.** Once you have those two numbers, both answers are one addition and one subtraction away.

2. **If given distance + time, divide FIRST to get speed.** Never put distances into the (D±U)/2 formula.

3. **Boat = (D+U)/2, Stream = (D–U)/2.** Notice the boat gets the **plus**, the stream gets the **minus**. Memory hook: the **b**oat is **b**igger, so it gets the bigger operation.

4. **Sanity checks that catch almost every error:**
   - Boat speed is always **greater** than stream speed (otherwise you could never go upstream)
   - Downstream speed is always **greater** than upstream speed
   - If you get a negative upstream speed, the boat can't move against the current at all

5. **Upstream time = k × downstream time → b : s = (k+1) : (k–1).**

6. **A round trip is never "distance × 2 ÷ still-water speed."** The slow leg dominates. The average is always **less** than b.

## 🎯 QUICK RECAP — say these out loud

- **Downstream = b + s** (river helps) &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Upstream = b – s** (river fights)
- **Boat = (D + U)/2** &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Stream = (D – U)/2**
- Given distance and time? **Divide first to get the speed.**
- Upstream time = k × downstream time → **b : s = (k+1) : (k–1)**
- Round trip average = **(b² – s²)/b**, always less than b
- Boat speed > stream speed, and downstream > upstream. **Always.**
