## 🎯 The Strategy (read this first — 2 minutes)

You cannot master 20 topics in one day. But you **can** score 70–80% by being smart about it.

Here's the truth about placement aptitude tests:

| Category | % of paper (typical) | Effort needed | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic (Number system, %, P&L, SI/CI, Avg, Work, STD, Trains, Boats, Ages) | ~45% | High | 🔴 **DO FIRST** |
| Logical Reasoning (Direction, Series, Blood Relation, Analogy, Seating) | ~25% | **LOW — easiest marks** | 🟢 **DO SECOND** |
| English (Grammar + Comprehension) | ~20% | Low | 🟢 **DO SECOND** |
| Data Interpretation | ~10% | Medium | 🟡 DO THIRD |

> ### ⚡ The single most important insight
> **Logical Reasoning + English = ~45% of the paper and needs the LEAST study time.**
> Most students burn all their time on maths and run out of time for reasoning. Don't do that.
> Reasoning topics (Blood Relations, Direction, Analogy, Alphabet Series) can each be learned in **20 minutes** and you'll get almost every question right.

## 🧠 HOW TO STUDY EACH FILE (the method that actually works)

Every file is built in the same 5 parts. Use them like this:

```
1. CONCEPT        → read once, slowly.                    (5 min)
2. FORMULAS       → write them on a separate sheet BY HAND. (3 min)
3. WORKED EXAMPLE → cover the solution. Try it yourself.
                    Then compare with my steps.            (15 min)
4. SHORTCUTS      → read twice. This is where speed comes from. (3 min)
5. PRACTICE       → do them. Check answers at the bottom.  (15 min)
```

> ⚠️ **The #1 mistake:** reading solutions and thinking "yes I understood that."
> Understanding ≠ ability to solve. **You must write the steps with a pen.** Keep one rough notebook open all day.

## ✅ EXAM-HALL TACTICS (worth 10+ marks by itself)

1. **Do 3 passes over the paper.**
   - Pass 1: answer everything you can solve in under 30 seconds. (Reasoning + English live here.)
   - Pass 2: the 1–2 minute calculations.
   - Pass 3: the long ones (DI, seating puzzles) with whatever time is left.

2. **Never spend more than 90 seconds on one question.** Mark it, move on. A skipped question costs 1 mark; a stuck question costs 5.

3. **Check for negative marking before you start.**
   - No negative marking → **attempt every single question.** Never leave a blank.
   - Negative marking → guess only when you've eliminated at least 2 options.

4. **Use the options.** It's an MCQ, not a written exam. Plug options into the question — often 10× faster than solving forward. This is especially true for Ages, Number System, and Work problems.

5. **Approximate in DI.** If options are far apart, round the numbers. 4,983 → 5,000.

6. **Start reasoning first if allowed.** Banking your easy marks early kills exam anxiety and protects you from running out of time.

7. **Read English questions last-but-not-least.** Comprehension eats time. Do grammar/one-liners first, passage last.
