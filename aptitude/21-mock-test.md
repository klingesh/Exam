## SECTION A — ARITHMETIC (Questions 1–20)

**1.** What is the unit digit of 7⁹⁵?
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 7 (d) 9

**2.** Find the HCF of 72 and 120.
(a) 12 (b) 18 (c) 24 (d) 36

**3.** What is 35% of 480?
(a) 148 (b) 158 (c) 168 (d) 178

**4.** A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above cost price and then gives a discount of 25%. What is his profit percent?
(a) 5% (b) 10% (c) 15% (d) 15.5%

**5.** Find the simple interest on ₹7,200 at 5% per annum for 3 years.
(a) ₹980 (b) ₹1,080 (c) ₹1,180 (d) ₹1,280

**6.** Find the compound interest on ₹6,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years.
(a) ₹1,200 (b) ₹1,260 (c) ₹1,320 (d) ₹1,360

**7.** Find the difference between compound interest and simple interest on ₹12,000 at 5% per annum for 2 years.
(a) ₹25 (b) ₹30 (c) ₹35 (d) ₹40

**8.** What is the average of the first 15 odd numbers?
(a) 13 (b) 14 (c) 15 (d) 16

**9.** A can complete a work in 12 days and B in 24 days. How long will they take working together?
(a) 6 days (b) 8 days (c) 9 days (d) 10 days

**10.** If 18 men can complete a work in 24 days, how many days will 27 men take?
(a) 14 days (b) 15 days (c) 16 days (d) 18 days

**11.** A train 240 m long, running at 72 km/h, crosses a platform in 25 seconds. Find the length of the platform.
(a) 240 m (b) 250 m (c) 260 m (d) 280 m

**12.** A boat travels downstream at 18 km/h and upstream at 12 km/h. Find the speed of the stream.
(a) 2 km/h (b) 3 km/h (c) 4 km/h (d) 6 km/h

**13.** A man travels from A to B at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. Find his average speed for the whole journey.
(a) 45 km/h (b) 48 km/h (c) 50 km/h (d) 52 km/h

**14.** A father is 3 times as old as his son. After 10 years, he will be twice as old as his son. Find the son's present age.
(a) 8 years (b) 10 years (c) 12 years (d) 15 years

**15.** Two dice are thrown together. What is the probability that the sum is 10?
(a) 1/9 (b) 1/12 (c) 1/6 (d) 5/36

**16.** A bag contains 6 red and 4 white balls. Two balls are drawn at random. What is the probability that both are red?
(a) 1/3 (b) 2/5 (c) 3/10 (d) 1/2

**17.** The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percent must a family reduce its consumption so that expenditure remains unchanged?
(a) 16.67% (b) 20% (c) 25% (d) 33.33%

**18.** Two successive discounts of 10% and 20% are given on an article. Find the single equivalent discount.
(a) 26% (b) 28% (c) 30% (d) 32%

**19.** The average of 8 numbers is 21. If one of the numbers, 15, is replaced by 27, what is the new average?
(a) 21.5 (b) 22 (c) 22.5 (d) 23

**20.** How many trailing zeros are there in 75!?
(a) 15 (b) 16 (c) 18 (d) 20

## Full solutions

### Section A — Arithmetic

**1. (b) 3** — Cycle of 7 is (7, 9, 3, 1), length 4. 95 ÷ 4 = 23 remainder 3 → 3rd item → **3**

**2. (c) 24** — 72 = 2³ × 3², 120 = 2³ × 3 × 5. Common primes at the lowest power: 2³ × 3 = **24**

**3. (c) 168** — 480 × 35/100 = 168. (Faster: 10% = 48, so 30% = 144; 5% = 24; 144 + 24 = **168**)

**4. (a) 5%** — Multiply the multipliers: 1.40 × 0.75 = 1.05 → **5% profit**

**5. (b) ₹1,080** — 7200 × 5 × 3/100 = 108000/100 = **1,080**

**6. (b) ₹1,260** — Amount = 6000 × 1.21 = 7,260. CI = 7260 – 6000 = **1,260**

**7. (b) ₹30** — CI – SI (2 yrs) = P(R/100)² = 12000 × 0.0025 = **30**

**8. (c) 15** — The average of the first n odd numbers is n. So **15**. (Check: sum = 15² = 225, 225/15 = 15 ✔)

**9. (b) 8 days** — LCM(12, 24) = 24 units. A = 2/day, B = 1/day, total 3/day → 24/3 = **8 days**

**10. (c) 16 days** — 18 × 24 = 27 × D → D = 432/27 = **16**. (More men, fewer days ✔)

**11. (c) 260 m** — 72 km/h = 20 m/s. Total distance in 25 s = 500 m. Platform = 500 – 240 = **260 m**

**12. (b) 3 km/h** — Stream = (D – U)/2 = (18 – 12)/2 = **3 km/h**

**13. (b) 48 km/h** — Equal distances → 2xy/(x+y) = 2(40)(60)/100 = 4800/100 = **48**. (Not 50!)

**14. (b) 10 years** — 3S + 10 = 2(S + 10) → 3S + 10 = 2S + 20 → S = **10**. (Father is 30; after 10 years, 40 and 20 ✔)

**15. (b) 1/12** — Sum 10 comes from (4,6), (5,5), (6,4) → 3 ways out of 36 → 3/36 = **1/12**

**16. (a) 1/3** — C(6,2)/C(10,2) = 15/45 = **1/3**

**17. (b) 20%** — 25/(100 + 25) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = **20%**

**18. (b) 28%** — 10 + 20 – (10×20)/100 = 30 – 2 = **28%**. (Check: 100 → 90 → 72 ✔)

**19. (c) 22.5** — Total = 8 × 21 = 168. Replacing 15 with 27 adds 12 → 180. New average = 180/8 = **22.5**

**20. (c) 18** — 75 ÷ 5 = 15, 75 ÷ 25 = 3, 75 ÷ 125 = 0. Total = 15 + 3 = **18**

### Section B — Logical Reasoning

**21. (d) West** — East → (left) North → (left) **West**

**22. (a) 10 km** — √(6² + 8²) = √100 = **10 km**. (The 6-8-10 triplet.)

**23. (b) 4 km West** — The 7 km South and 7 km North cancel; only 4 km West remains

**24. (c) P** — K = 11, so 27 – 11 = 16 = **P**

**25. (c) R** — Positions 4, 6, 9, 13. Gaps: +2, +3, +4 → next gap +5 → 13 + 5 = 18 = **R**

**26. (b) OQ** — First letters C(3), G(7), K(11) → +4 each → O(15). Each pair skips one letter (C→E), so O→Q = **OQ**

**27. (a) CPPL** — The rule is +1 (P→Q, E→F, N→O…). So B→C, O→P, O→P, K→L = **CPPL**

**28. (b) U** — 26 – 6 + 1 = 21 = **U**

**29. (c) Father** — Her father's father = her grandfather. The grandfather's only son must be her own **father**

**30. (c) Aunt** — A is the sister of C's father, so A is C's paternal **aunt**

**31. (b) Painter** — A book is created by an author; a painting is created by a **painter**

**32. (c) 64** — 5² = 25, so 8² = **64**

**33. (b) Earthquake** — A barometer measures pressure; a seismograph measures **earthquakes**

### Section C — English

**34. (a) is** — "Each" is always singular

**35. (b) for** — "Three days" is a duration → **for**. ("Since" needs a point in time.)

**36. (c) of** — "Afraid **of**" is the fixed pair

**37. (b) An** — "MBA" is pronounced "em-bee-ay", starting with a vowel **sound** → **an**

**38. (b) about the problem** — "Discuss" takes no preposition. It should be "discussed the problem"

**39. (b) A house is being built by them** — Present continuous active → "is being + V₃" in the passive

**40. (b) Outdated** — "Obsolete" means no longer in use, **outdated**
