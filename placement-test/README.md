## 🧠 How to use an outline file

Each one has the same three parts:

```
1. WHAT'S IN THIS SECTION   → the topic structure, at a glance
2. TOPIC-BY-TOPIC OUTLINE   → sub-points under each topic
3. ⭐ IMPORTANT TO NOTE      → the high-priority items and key terms
```

**Suggested approach:**
1. Read your stream's outline once to see the full scope.
2. Mark the ⭐ items — those are where marks concentrate.
3. Expand only those into proper notes or revise them from your class material.
4. For everything else, knowing the definition and where it fits is usually enough.

> ⚠️ **Marketing note:** the PDF says to also prepare *"the syllabus of Marketing Management covered in the MBA Syllabus"* in addition to the 9 listed topics. The outline covers the 9 listed topics; if your college issued a separate Marketing Management syllabus, check it alongside.
