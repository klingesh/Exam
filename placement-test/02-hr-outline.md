## TOPIC-BY-TOPIC OUTLINE

### 1. Basic Introduction to HRM ⭐⭐
- **Definition and scope** of HRM
- **Evolution** — Personnel Management → HRM → Strategic HRM (know the direction of travel)
- **Goals and objectives** of HRM
- **Importance** of HRM in organizational success
- **Ethical considerations** in HRM
- **The HR department's role** in the organization
- **Stakeholders** — employees, managers, unions, government, shareholders

### 2. Abbreviations & General Terms ⭐⭐⭐
> This is a pure-recall topic and therefore easy marks. Learn the list.
- **Common abbreviations** — HRIS, ATS, L&D, KPI, EEOC
- **Key terminology** — job analysis, competency, succession planning, talent management
- **HR metrics and analytics** — attrition rate, cost per hire, time to hire, absenteeism
- **Legal & compliance terms** — FLSA, ADA, FMLA
- **Industry-specific HR terms**

### 3. HRM Planning, Roles & Responsibilities ⭐⭐⭐
- **Strategic HR planning** — aligning HR with organizational goals
- **Workforce planning and forecasting** — demand and supply of labour
- **Job analysis** → outputs are **job description** and **job specification**
- **Job design** — job rotation, enlargement, enrichment
- **HR roles** — Recruiter, HR Generalist, HR Specialist, HR Manager, VP of HR, CHRO
- **HR responsibilities** — administrative / operational / strategic (the three-level split)
- **HR department structure and organization**

### 4. Hierarchy ⭐⭐
- **Organizational structures and HR implications**
- **Levels of HR management** — entry / mid / senior
- **Reporting relationships and communication channels**
- **Span of control** and **delegation** — wide vs narrow span
- **Structure types** — matrix, flat, hierarchical (know the trade-offs)

### 5. Selection, Training & Recruitment ⭐⭐⭐
- **Recruitment sources** — internal vs external (know advantages of each)
- **Selection process** — application → screening → interviews → assessments → decision
- **Recruitment vs selection** — positive vs negative process
- **Types of interviews** — structured, unstructured, behavioural, panel
- **Onboarding and orientation**
- **TNA** (Training Needs Analysis) — organizational / task / person level
- **Training delivery methods** — classroom, online, on-the-job
- **Evaluation of training effectiveness** — Kirkpatrick's four levels: reaction → learning → behaviour → results
- **Employee development and career planning**

### 6. Compensation & Benefits ⭐⭐⭐
- **Compensation philosophies and strategies** — lead / match / lag the market
- **Job evaluation** methods — ranking, classification, point method, factor comparison
- **Salary structures** — grades and ranges
- **Types of compensation** — base pay, variable pay, incentives
- **Direct vs indirect compensation**
- **Employee benefits** — health insurance, retirement plans, paid time off
- **Legal and regulatory aspects** of compensation
- **Pay equity and fairness** — internal vs external equity

### 7. Performance Management ⭐⭐⭐
- **Appraisal methods** — 360-degree feedback, MBO, rating scales, BARS, ranking
- **Performance management vs performance appraisal** — continuous system vs periodic event
- **Setting performance standards and objectives** — SMART goals
- **Performance feedback and coaching**
- **PIP** — Performance Improvement Plan
- **Performance management software and systems**
- **Performance calibration** — normalising ratings across managers
- **Appraisal errors** — halo effect, recency, central tendency, leniency/strictness bias

### 8. Employee Relations ⭐⭐
- **Building positive employee relationships**
- **Communication and engagement strategies**
- **Employee motivation and morale** — Maslow, Herzberg two-factor theory (know these two at minimum)
- **Employee recognition and rewards** — monetary vs non-monetary
- **Managing diversity and inclusion**
- **Workplace culture**

### 9. Grievance Handling ⭐⭐
- **Grievance procedures and policies** — the step-ladder approach
- **Investigating and resolving** employee grievances
- **Mediation and conflict resolution**
- **Documentation and record keeping**
- **Preventative strategies** — open door policy, suggestion schemes

### 10. Dispute Resolution ⭐⭐
- **Types of workplace disputes** — interpersonal vs organizational
- **ADR methods** — conciliation, **mediation**, **arbitration**, adjudication
- **Mediation vs arbitration** — non-binding facilitation vs binding decision
- **Negotiation and conflict management skills** — Thomas–Kilmann styles: competing, collaborating, compromising, avoiding, accommodating
- **Legal considerations** in dispute resolution
- **Building a culture of conflict resolution**

### 11. Legal Requirements & Labour Law ⭐⭐
- **Employment laws** — minimum wage, overtime, discrimination
- **EEO** — Equal Employment Opportunity
- **Workplace safety and health** — OSHA
- **Labour unions and collective bargaining**
- **Employment contracts and agreements**
- **Data privacy in HR**
- 🇮🇳 *If your exam is India-focused, also know:* Industrial Disputes Act, Factories Act, Payment of Wages Act, Minimum Wages Act, EPF, ESI, Gratuity, and the four new Labour Codes

### 12. HR Policies ⭐
- **Developing and implementing** HR policies
- **Types** — attendance, leave, code of conduct, POSH/anti-harassment
- **Communicating and enforcing** policies
- **Policy review and updates**
- **Policy documentation**

### 13. AI in HR Functions ⭐⭐
- **AI in recruitment and selection** — AI-powered resume screening, chatbots
- **AI in training and development** — personalized learning paths, AI-driven coaching
- **AI in performance management** — predictive analytics, AI-based feedback
- **AI in engagement and retention** — sentiment analysis, AI-driven surveys, attrition prediction
- **Ethical considerations** — algorithmic bias, transparency, consent, data privacy
- **HRIS and AI integration**

### 14. Traditional vs Modern HR Models ⭐⭐⭐
> Almost certainly a comparison question. Prepare it as a table.
- Traditional HR (**Personnel Management**) vs Modern HR (**Strategic HR**)
- Administration focus vs **strategic partnership**
- **Transactional vs transformational** HR
- **Centralized vs decentralized** HR
- Role of **technology** in modern HR
- **Data-driven HR**

**Frameworks to know by name and content:**

| Framework | Content |
|---|---|
| **Evolution of HR** | Personnel Management → HRM → Strategic HRM |
| **Three HR responsibility levels** | Administrative → Operational → Strategic |
| **Selection process** | Application → screening → interview → assessment → offer |
| **Kirkpatrick's training evaluation** | Reaction → Learning → Behaviour → Results |
| **Job analysis outputs** | Job Description (the job) + Job Specification (the person) |
| **Job design techniques** | Rotation, Enlargement, Enrichment |
| **Herzberg two-factor** | Hygiene factors (prevent dissatisfaction) vs Motivators (create satisfaction) |
| **Maslow's hierarchy** | Physiological → Safety → Social → Esteem → Self-actualisation |
| **Thomas–Kilmann conflict styles** | Competing, Collaborating, Compromising, Avoiding, Accommodating |
| **ADR ladder** | Negotiation → Conciliation → Mediation → Arbitration → Litigation |
