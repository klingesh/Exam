## TOPIC-BY-TOPIC OUTLINE

### 1. Marketing Mix & Strategy ⭐⭐⭐
- **Marketing mix** — 4 Ps (Product, Price, Place, Promotion); 7 Ps for services (+ People, Process, Physical evidence)
- **Marketing planning process** — situation analysis → objectives → strategy → action plan → control
- **Value chain** — primary vs support activities; where value is added
- **Core competencies** — what makes a firm hard to imitate
- **Competitor analysis** — identifying competitors, assessing strengths, anticipating reactions
- **Competitor strategies** — market leader / challenger / follower / nicher
- **B2B vs B2C marketing** — the comparison table
- **BCG Matrix** — Star, Cash Cow, Question Mark, Dog (growth × market share)
- **Global marketing strategies** — standardization vs adaptation
- **Market entry strategies** — exporting, licensing, franchising, joint venture, wholly owned subsidiary

### 2. NPD, STP & Branding ⭐⭐⭐
- **New Product Development stages** — idea generation → screening → concept development & testing → business analysis → product development → test marketing → commercialization
- **STP** — Segmentation (geographic/demographic/psychographic/behavioural) → Targeting → Positioning
- **Brand positioning & differentiation** — positioning statement, points of difference vs points of parity
- **Brand personality** — brand as a set of human traits
- **Product vs brand extension** — line extension vs brand extension; risks of dilution
- **Product testing vs test marketing** — the difference matters
- **Test marketing** — advantages, disadvantages, and strategies (standard, controlled, simulated)
- **Launch cycle** and **PLC** — Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline; managing growth and maturity
- **Commercialization** — full-scale launch decisions (when, where, to whom, how)

### 3. IMC, Advertising & Pricing ⭐⭐
- **IMC — Integrated Marketing Communication** — coordinating all communication channels so the brand sends one consistent message
- **Communication mix elements** — advertising, sales promotion, PR/publicity, personal selling, direct & digital
- **Advertising vs sales promotion** — long-term brand building vs short-term sales push
- **Publicity vs public relations** — earned coverage vs managed reputation
- **Advertisement budget methods** — percentage of sales, affordable, competitive parity, objective-and-task
- **Advertising effectiveness** — pre-testing vs post-testing; reach, frequency, GRP
- **Marketing in the service economy** — service characteristics: intangibility, inseparability, variability, perishability; the service quality gap idea
- **Pricing strategies** — cost-plus, penetration, skimming, value-based, psychological, competitive, bundling

### 4. Marketing Information System & Research ⭐⭐
- **MIS components** — internal records, marketing intelligence, marketing research, analytics
- **Marketing research process** — define problem → design research → collect data → analyse → report findings
- **Data types** — primary vs secondary; qualitative vs quantitative
- **CRM** — meaning, objectives, operational/analytical/collaborative types, benefits

### 5. Consumer Behaviour ⭐⭐
- **Buyer decision process** — need recognition → information search → evaluation of alternatives → purchase → post-purchase behaviour
- **Factors influencing buying behaviour** — cultural, social, personal, psychological
- **Consumer behaviour models** — the general stimulus–response ("black box") idea, Howard–Sheth, Engel–Kollat–Blackwell
- **AIDA** — Attention → Interest → Desire → Action
- **Types of buying behaviour** — complex, dissonance-reducing, habitual, variety-seeking

### 6. Sales Management ⭐⭐
- **Selling process** — prospecting → pre-approach → approach → presentation → handling objections → closing → follow-up
- **Managing sales information** — sales reports, CRM data, MIS
- **Sales organization structures** — by geography, product, customer, or a hybrid
- **Sales territory management** — designing, allocating, and balancing territories
- **Sales quota** — types (volume, revenue, activity, profit) and purposes
- **Lead generation & prospect** — lead vs prospect vs qualified lead
- **Sales funnel** — awareness → interest → evaluation → decision → purchase
- **Inside sales vs field sales** — remote/telephonic vs on-site selling

### 7. Channel Management, Retailing & Wholesaling ⭐
- **Channel levels** — zero-level (direct) up to three-level
- **Channel functions** and **channel conflict** — horizontal vs vertical
- **Channel design & selection**; intensive vs selective vs exclusive distribution
- **Retailing** — store vs non-store formats; retail types
- **Wholesaling** — functions and types of wholesalers
- **Logistics in marketing** — order processing, warehousing, inventory, transportation

### 8. Digital Marketing ⭐⭐⭐
- **SEO** — on-page vs off-page; keywords, backlinks, organic vs paid
- **SMM** — platform-specific strategy, organic vs paid social
- **Email marketing** and **ORM** (Online Reputation Management)
- **Affiliate marketing** — commission-based partner promotion
- **PPC** — pay-per-click; how bidding and ad auctions work
- **CLV** — Customer Lifetime Value; why retention beats acquisition
- **Influencer marketing** — macro vs micro vs nano influencers

### 9. MarTech, Content & Email ⭐⭐
- **MarTech** — the marketing technology stack; automation tools
- **Social and search engine advertising** — targeting options, budgeting, bidding
- **Social listening** — monitoring brand mentions and sentiment
- **Planning, targeting and budgeting** for social campaigns
- **Lead generation and audience aggregation** on social media; trends in social media marketing
- **Inbound marketing** — attract, engage, delight (vs outbound/interruption marketing)
- **Content marketing** — content in the digital era, **UGC** (user-generated content), structuring campaigns and daily content calendars
- **Email marketing** — types (newsletter, promotional, transactional, drip), tools, and measures (open rate, CTR, conversion, bounce, unsubscribe)
