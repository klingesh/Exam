## UNIT-BY-UNIT OUTLINE

### Unit 1 — Logistics & SCM Fundamentals ⭐⭐⭐
- **Definitions** of logistics management and supply chain management
- **Logistics vs SCM** ⭐ — logistics is a part of SCM; SCM is broader and inter-organizational
- **Evolution** and **importance**
- **Key drivers** of SCM and logistics relationships
- **SCOR model** (Supply Chain Operations Reference) — its processes: **Plan, Source, Make, Deliver, Return** (some versions add Enable)
- The **7 Rs** of logistics idea — right product, quantity, condition, place, time, customer, cost

### Unit 2 — Transportation ⭐⭐⭐
- **Basics of transportation**; transportation **functionality and principles**
- **Multimodal transport** — one contract, more than one mode
- **Modal characteristics** and **modal comparisons** — road, rail, air, water, pipeline compared on cost, speed, capacity, flexibility, reliability
- **International air cargo transport**
- **Coastal and ocean transportation**; characteristics of shipping transport
- **Types of ships** — container ships, bulk carriers, tankers, RoRo, general cargo, reefer

### Unit 3 — Containerization & ICD ⭐⭐⭐
- **Containerization** — genesis, concept, **classification**, **benefits and constraints**
- Container sizes — **TEU** (twenty-foot equivalent unit) and FEU
- **ICD** (Inland Container Depot) — roles and functions
- **CFS** (Container Freight Station) — and how it differs from an ICD
- **Export clearance at ICD**
- **CONCOR** (Container Corporation of India) and ICDs under CONCOR

### Unit 4 — Packing & Packaging ⭐⭐
- **Packing vs packaging** ⭐ — the distinction is commonly asked
- **Meaning, functions and essentials**
- **Packing for storage**, overseas shipment, inland transportation, **product content protection**
- **Packaging levels** — **primary, secondary, tertiary** ⭐
- Requirements of **consumer packaging**, **channel member packaging**, **transport packaging**
- **Shrink packaging**
- **Identification codes, bar codes** and **EDI** (Electronic Data Interchange)
- **UPC** (Universal Product Code) and **GS1 standards**
- **Package labels** and **symbols** used on packages

### Unit 5 — Export & Import Logistics ⭐⭐⭐
**Export flow:**
- **Picking** → **packing** → **vessel booking** → **customs** → **documentation** → **shipment** → **delivery** to distribution centres, distributors, then retail outlets
- **LCL vs FCL** ⭐ — Less-than Container Load vs Full Container Load

**Import flow:**
- **Documents collection** → **valuing** → **bonded warehousing** → **customs formalities** → **clearing** → distribution to units

## ADDITIONAL TOPICS

### 1. Multi-Modal Transport & Logistics Management ⭐⭐
- Multi-modal transport systems; shipping & logistics management
- **3PL** (Third-Party Logistics) — outsourced logistics execution
- **4PL** (Fourth-Party Logistics) — manages the whole supply chain, including other 3PLs
- Know **1PL → 2PL → 3PL → 4PL** as a progression

### 2. Demand Forecasting ⭐⭐
- **Qualitative methods** — Delphi, expert opinion, market research, sales force estimates
- **Quantitative methods** — moving average, exponential smoothing, regression, time series
- **AI & machine learning** in demand forecasting

### 3. Inventory Management ⭐⭐⭐
- **Inventory types** — raw material, WIP, finished goods, MRO
- **Control techniques**
- **EOQ** — Economic Order Quantity ⭐ (know the formula, see below)
- **Safety stock** and **reorder point**
- **JIT** (Just-In-Time) and **lean inventory**
- **Inventory optimization techniques**
- **ABC analysis** (by value) and **XYZ analysis** (by demand variability) ⭐

### 4. Warehouse Management ⭐⭐
- **Functions of warehouses** — storage, consolidation, break-bulk, cross-docking
- **Types** — private, public, **bonded**, automated
- **Role & responsibilities** of a warehouse manager
- **WMS** (Warehouse Management System)
- **Automation** — robotics, **AS/RS** (Automated Storage and Retrieval System)
- **Cold chain logistics** and specialized storage

### 5. Sustainability in Supply Chain ⭐⭐
- **Green logistics** and **reverse logistics** ⭐ (reverse logistics = returns, repairs, recycling flowing backwards)
- **Carbon footprint reduction** techniques
- **Circular supply chain** management
- **Regulatory compliance** for sustainability

## ⭐ IMPORTANT TO NOTE

### Formulas
```
EOQ = √( 2DS / H )
        D = annual demand
        S = ordering cost per order
        H = holding (carrying) cost per unit per year

Reorder Point = (Average daily usage × Lead time) + Safety stock

Inventory Turnover = Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory
```
